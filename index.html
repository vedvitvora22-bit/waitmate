What WaitMate Solves

Tap or hover any card to inspect how each part of the queue works.

Live ETA Score

Realistic arrival times from party size, queue length, and table turnover.

Your ETA is estimated from the live queue length ahead of you, the active party sizes (a party of 6 takes longer than a party of 2), and the venue's average table turnover speed. Groq AI rush adjustments nudge the estimate up during detected rush spikes — so the number you see is the number you get.

Reliability Grade (A–D)

Historical on-time seating accuracy and low wait-time variance.

Reliability reflects historical on-time seating accuracy — how often the restaurant seats diners within the promised ETA. It weighs verified "I've Arrived" check-ins and favors venues with low wait-time variance. A 94% Grade A score means the wait you're quoted is the wait you'll get.

Verified "I've Arrived" Gate

Hosts only seat remote diners once they tap "I've Arrived" at the venue.

When you join remotely, your rank is locked — but the host only seats you after you tap "I've Arrived" at the venue entrance. This confirms your physical presence before a table is assigned, so remote joiners can't hold tables they never show up for and walk-in guests aren't skipped unfairly. Every check-in also feeds the Reliability Score, keeping the whole network honest.

+5 Mins & Instant Cancel Controls

One-tap status updates prevent queue bottlenecks.

Stuck in traffic? Tap "+5 Mins" to gracefully delay your turn without losing your place. Can't make it? Instant Cancel frees the table for the next diner immediately. Both updates reflect on the Host Stand in real time.
